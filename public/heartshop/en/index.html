Before you buy

How much is delivery and how long does it take?

In this demo, example options are InPost Paczkomat (parcel locker) PLN 13.99, InPost courier PLN 16.99. Orders from PLN 150.00 ship free. The example dispatch time is 1–2 working days; no physical product will be shipped.

Can I return the box?

Yes. You have 14 days from receiving the parcel to withdraw from the contract without giving a reason. Details are in the terms.

Do you issue invoices?

Yes. In the order, tick “I need a company invoice” and enter your NIP (Polish tax ID). The invoice arrives by email.

How can I pay?

With BLIK, Google Pay, Apple Pay or a fast bank transfer. Payments are handled by Paymove.
